Coaching FAQ

Is coaching the same as therapy?
No. Coaching is future- and goal-focused and does not diagnose or treat mental-health conditions. If you're struggling with your mental health, please see a licensed professional. We can point you to therapy resources on request.
How are coaches vetted?
We check training credentials (for example ICF or equivalent), references, insurance where applicable, and review a sample session before a coach joins the network.
Why is it free for me?
Coaches pay Life.Studio a referral or listing fee. You pay the coach's standard rate — never more.
What if I don't like my matches?
Reply to your match email and we'll re-match you, as many times as needed.
