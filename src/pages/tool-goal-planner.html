Goal-setting that actually works

Decades of research on goal-setting theory (Locke & Latham) show that specific, challenging goals beat "do your best" goals. Pair that with implementation intentions — "if X happens, then I'll do Y" — studied by Peter Gollwitzer, and you close the gap between intending and doing. Read more in SMART goals vs. systems.
