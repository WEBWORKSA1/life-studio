    How to read your Wheel of Life

    The Wheel of Life is a classic coaching exercise, widely credited to Paul J. Meyer, that turns a vague feeling ("something is off") into a picture you can act on. The shape matters as much as the average: a big, lopsided wheel rolls badly, and a small, round one rolls slowly.

    • Average below 5: focus on one foundation area — usually health or money — before anything else.
    • Big gap between highest and lowest: your strongest area is often "borrowing" energy from your weakest. Rebalance deliberately.
    • Everything 7+: you're ready for stretch goals. Pick the area with the most upside, not the lowest score.
