Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

Summary

Tool data (scores, habits, goals, birth date) is stored only in your browser's local storage and is never sent to us. We receive personal information only when you submit a form.

What we collect

  • Form submissions: name, email, and the details you choose to share (e.g. coaching goals, budget). Forms are processed by our form-delivery provider (FormSubmit) and delivered to our private inbox.
  • Analytics (only with consent): aggregated usage data via Google Analytics.
  • Advertising: we use Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

How we use it

  • To answer you, send what you requested (reports, newsletters) and match you with coaches when you ask us to.
  • With your explicit consent, coaching requests are shared with up to 3 matched coaches.
  • We never sell your personal information.

Your rights

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or withdraw consent at any time, by contacting us. Residents of the EU/UK (GDPR), California (CCPA/CPRA), Canada (PIPEDA) and India (DPDP Act) have additional rights, which we honour.

Cookies

Essential storage remembers your theme and consent choice. Analytics and ad cookies load only after you accept. Change your choice via "Cookie settings" in the footer.

Children

Life.Studio is not directed to children under 16, and challenges are for adults 18+.
