Why a life calendar works

Popularised by Tim Urban's essay "Your Life in Weeks", the life calendar makes time concrete. Most people overestimate how much time they have for "someday" plans and underestimate what a focused year can change. Seeing roughly 4,000 weeks at once shifts the question from "What should I do eventually?" to "What deserves the next 12 weeks?"

Try this: circle the next 12 weeks and commit to one meaningful project inside them. Our goal planner will break it into milestones.
