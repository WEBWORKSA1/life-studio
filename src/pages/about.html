Our mission

Life.Studio exists to make intentional living practical. Designers use a studio to sketch, test and iterate. We think your life deserves the same treatment: honest assessment, small experiments, feedback, and steady improvement.

We build free tools you can use in minutes, publish guides grounded in research rather than hype, connect people with vetted coaches when they want to go faster, and run challenges that make change social — and fun.

Our principles

  • Practical over preachy. Every page ends in an action.
  • Evidence over hype. We cite research and flag uncertainty.
  • Private by default. Tool data stays in your browser.
  • Transparent money. We tell you how we earn: ads, sponsors, coach partnerships and reader support.
