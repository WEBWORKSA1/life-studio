The math behind financial independence

Your freedom number is annual spending divided by a safe withdrawal rate. At 4% — a rule of thumb derived from historical US market studies such as the Trinity study — that's 25× annual spending. The single biggest lever is your savings rate: it both grows the portfolio and shrinks the target. Read Financial independence basics.
