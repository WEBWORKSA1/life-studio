FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Life.Studio really free?
Yes. All tools and guides are free. We're funded by respectful advertising, sponsorships, coach partnerships and reader support.
Where is my data stored?
Tool data (scores, habits, goals) is saved only in your own browser. We only receive what you choose to submit in a form.
How does coach matching work?
You answer a few questions, a match specialist reviews them and introduces you to up to 3 coaches who fit your goal, budget and style. There is no charge and no obligation.
Can I contribute or partner?
Absolutely — see Careers, For coaches and Advertise.
